Transparent Service Scope & Pass-Through Breakdown

We believe in complete transparency. Here is exactly how domain ownership, hosting tiers, third-party subscriptions, and major project scopes are handled.

🌐

Domain Name Ownership & Administration

  • Option A (Best Practice — Client Owned): Client purchases & retains 100% direct domain ownership on their registrar (Namecheap, Cloudflare, Google), delegating admin DNS access to us ($0 fee).
  • Option B (Managed Re-billing): If we register & manage the domain for you, it is billed annually at direct registrar cost + standard $15–$25/year for DNS administration.
  • SSL & DNSSEC Security (Included): Free automated SSL/TLS encryption certificates, DNSSEC setup, and Cloudflare CDN proxy routing included on all managed domains ($0 extra).
⚡

Hosting Tiers & Workspace Emails

  • Base Hosting Included: Shared static edge CDN hosting (up to 5 GB storage / basic traffic) is included at $0 extra within your $199/mo care plan.
  • Dedicated / E-Commerce Add-Ons: Dedicated VPS servers, e-commerce engines (Shopify, WooCommerce), or managed WP Engine/Kinsta environments listed as separate line items (+$30 to $100+/mo).
  • Email & Workspaces: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or custom email hosting billed directly pass-through at cost.
🔌

Plugins, APIs & Graphic Revisions

  • Premium Plugins & APIs: Commercial WP plugins (Elementor Pro, Form builders), paid booking engines, or external API licenses billed pass-through at direct provider cost.
  • Branding & Logo Revisions: Initial builds include structured revision rounds (2–3 rounds). Full rebrands, new vector logo marks, or extensive graphic design carry dedicated one-time fees.
  • Custom Code First Strategy: We write clean, lightweight custom code whenever possible to avoid unnecessary commercial plugin subscriptions, saving you recurring software fees.
🚚

Site Rebuilds & Data Migrations

  • Initial Build Prerequisite: All $199/mo care plans require an initial One-Time Project Build (Landing Page Sprint or Full Web Studio Package) upfront to cover initial design & custom engineering before subscribing to the monthly maintenance retainer.
  • Maintenance vs Full Rebuilds: Your $199/mo plan covers ongoing care, security, and minor content updates. Complete site redesigns, new page additions, or structural overhauls quoted as separate initial project fees.
  • Data & Mail Migrations: Transferring legacy websites, complex databases, or corporate mail servers from old providers quoted as one-time migration services.
