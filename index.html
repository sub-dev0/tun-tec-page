Terms of Service

End User Agreement

Last updated: August 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing Tundra Tech's website, requesting project proposals, or purchasing web development, cloud hosting, or monthly maintenance plans, you ("Client") agree to these terms.

2. Scope of Web Services

Tundra Tech offers custom website development, product pages, logo design, landing page sprints, cloud hosting, domain administration, and monthly care retainers.

3. Intellectual Property Ownership

Upon full payment of invoice balances, Client receives complete ownership of final custom website designs, copy, and client-specific code deliverables.

4. Maintenance & Uptime SLA

Clients subscribed to monthly website care plans receive 24/7 uptime monitoring, security patching, and regular backups.

5. Domain Ownership & Administration

Clients retain 100% direct ownership of domain names registered under their registrar account. Delegated DNS access carries a $0 fee. Managed domains registered by us are billed annually at direct cost plus a standard $15–$25/yr DNS administration fee. Automated SSL certificates and DNSSEC security routing are included at $0 extra.

6. Hosting, Email & Pass-Through Costs

Monthly care plans include base shared static edge CDN hosting (up to 5 GB storage). Dedicated VPS servers, e-commerce engines, corporate workspace email (Google Workspace/Microsoft 365), commercial plugin subscriptions, and external API licenses are billed directly pass-through at cost.

7. Project Scope, Rebuilds & Migrations

Care retainers cover ongoing site care, security defense, and minor content/layout tweaks. Initial website builds, complete site redesigns, major structural overhauls, graphic rebrands, and legacy data/mail migrations are quoted separately as one-time project fees.
