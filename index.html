We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us for managed IT support, cloud administration, cybersecurity audits, web development, or monthly care services. This includes:

  • Inquiry messages submitted through our proposal request forms

Information provided is used strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Responding to your inquiries and delivering customized MSP & web service estimates

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal or organizational information to third parties. All submissions are protected using modern encryption standards (HTTPS/TLS) and secure infrastructure.

Terms of Service

End User Agreement

Last updated: August 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing Tundra Tech's website, requesting proposals, or subscribing to managed IT support, cloud hosting, or monthly MSP care plans, you ("Client") agree to these terms.

2. Scope of Remote MSP & Web Services

Tundra Tech offers 100% remote Managed Service Provider solutions, including remote IT helpdesk, cloud & SaaS administration (Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace), endpoint security, automated backups, custom web design, high-speed cloud hosting, and monthly upkeep retainers.

3. Intellectual Property Ownership

Upon full payment of invoice balances, Client receives complete ownership of final custom website designs, copy, and client-specific code deliverables.

4. Maintenance & Uptime SLA

Clients subscribed to monthly MSP and website care plans receive 24/7 telemetry monitoring, proactive security patching, and automated cloud backups under the agreed SLA response tiers.

5. Domain Ownership & Administration

Clients retain 100% direct ownership of domain names registered under their registrar account. Delegated DNS access carries no additional fee. Managed domains registered by us are billed annually at direct cost plus a standard pass-through DNS administration fee. Automated SSL certificates and DNSSEC security routing are included at no additional cost.

6. Hosting, Email & Pass-Through Costs

Monthly plans include base static edge CDN hosting (up to 5 GB storage). Dedicated VPS servers, e-commerce database engines, corporate workspace licenses (Google Workspace/Microsoft 365), commercial plugin subscriptions, and external API fees are billed directly pass-through at cost.

7. Project Scope, Rebuilds & Migrations

Monthly retainers cover ongoing IT care, endpoint defense, and standard content/layout upkeep. Initial custom builds, major cloud migrations, full infrastructure overhauls, and graphic rebrands are quoted separately as one-time project sprints.
